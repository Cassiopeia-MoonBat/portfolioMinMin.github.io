Faceless Cultivation

A point and click puzzle game about fufilling customer wishes by creating masks and reparing them

Godot 2026

Project Description

You own a small mask shop and are passionate about building masks. Your main customers? A variety of occult sects and associations. Your task? Build your customers their desired mask and repair them if they break during ominous rituals. How well can you fulfill your customers' wishes to transform them from a normal citizen into a passionate cultist? Oh, what's that? A mask with parts missing? And you have to rebuild it exactly as it was. What parts did you use again...?

Created during the Global Game Jam 2026, at the "GGJ goes THE LÄND: Südbaden games" site, Faceless Cultivation is a game prototype based on the prompt "mask". The Ideation and concept development for this prototype was a Team effort. My contribution to the project was primarily the creation of concept art and 2D assets. I was also part of the the narrative design and general world building. The prototype wont be continued, but is playable on the Global Game Jam website.
