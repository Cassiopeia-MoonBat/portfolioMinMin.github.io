About Me

▾

Hi, my name is Mina. I’m a creative developer passionate about art, interactive media, games, and storytelling. Currently studying "Games and Immersive Media" at HS-Furtwangen also known as the "Blackforest University". My course of study teaches a lot of different basics used to produce games and interactive media. This includes but is not limited to game design, 2D and 3D art and its animation, sound design and coding. I currently am focusing on concept art and 2D character design (both visually and narratively), but I am also exploring and improving other areas of interest such as coding and narrative design.

I was able to showcase my work, "Those Who Remained", at Gamescom 2025 as part of the RawTalentBooth! Now in 2026, I am not only showcasing at Gamescom at RawTalentBooth, but also am happy to announce that I contributed to the RawTalentBooth as colead of the "graphics force". My tasks as colead include the overseeing of the graphics team, as well as the management and communication between the graphics team and the other teams of the RawTalentBooth. This position also enables me to become the lead in next years Gamescom, making me responsible for the management of the graphics part for the booth as well as taking notes on the process and teaching the colead for the following year.
