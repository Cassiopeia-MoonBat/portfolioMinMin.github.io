Hush Darling

Deep themed Point and Click adventure (Trigger Warning: Domestic Violence, Femicide)

Unity 2026

Project Description

"Hush, Darling" is a psychological experience that starts with a deceptively cute facade, only to slowly reveal a dark reality. The game follows a young woman trying to hide the physical and emotional abuse of her relationship behind makeup. By breaking through the censorship and silence that often surrounds domestic violence and femicide, the game refuses to sugarcoat the situation, forcing players to confront a harsh reality that society too often ignores.

This game was originallycreated by a team of 5 students at the Global Game Jam 2026. I joined thesde 5 as a manager and coordinator, contributing to the showcasing of the game at Gamescom 2026, again as part of the Raw Talent booth. I also contributed by creating one piece of concept art as well as giving my opinion and constructive critique.
