Those Who Remained

A first-person, escape room puzzle game, where you are locked into an old mansion, searching for files while also trying to escape

Unreal Engine 5 2025

Project Description

"Those Who Remained" was developed as part of the "Entry Project" – a module that gives the students two weeks at the beginning of the studies to learn the Unreal Engine and then to develop a level in the span of four weeks total - as part of the course of study "Games and Immersive Media" at "Hochschule Furtwangen". In my case, the result is a light escape room game in which you can solve puzzles and explore a house with the help of magic mushrooms. We as a team even managed to present our very first game project at Gamescom 2025 as part of the "RAW TALENT booth" and by doing so, getting officially labeld USK 12 for this game. By exhibiting we wanted to show how much you can achieve as a complete beginner in just a few weeks.

My contributions include Project Management, the level- and environment- design, Programming (in form of Blueprints) and Sound Design
